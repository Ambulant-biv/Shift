Convert any file. Keep it on your computer.

Shift turns photos, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, drawings, video and audio into the format you need. Everything happens on your Windows PC; nothing is uploaded.

Download for Windows

Free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

What Shift converts

Add files or a whole folder, pick the format you want, and Shift does the rest. Your originals are never changed or overwritten.

Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are converted with Microsoft Office, or LibreOffice if that is what you have. Everything else works without extra software.

PDF drawings back into CAD

Got a floor plan as a PDF? Shift turns it into DXF or DWG at the scale you choose, from 1:1 to 1:1000. Lines, filled areas and text each get their own layer, so you can carry on in AutoCAD, BricsCAD or DraftSight.

Works for PDFs exported from drawing software. A scanned drawing is only a picture, so there are no lines to convert.

The PDF tools you would otherwise look for online

  • Merge photos, PDFs and documents into one PDF, in the order you choose.
  • Split into single pages, every few pages, or the ranges you type.
  • Rotate all pages, or only the ones that are upside down.
  • Keep the pages you need, or remove the ones you don’t.
  • Compress for email: a 5.3 MB inspection report became 483 KB in our test.

Your files never leave your computer.

  • Shift works offline. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.
  • The only thing it asks the internet is whether a new version is out, once a day. You can switch that off in Settings.
  • The source code is public, so anyone can check what Shift does.

Questions

Is Shift really free?

Yes. Shift is open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. There are no ads, no account and no paid version.

Do I need Microsoft Office?

Only for converting Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. LibreOffice works too. Images, PDFs, drawings, video and audio need nothing extra.

Which languages does Shift speak?

English, Dutch and German. Shift follows the language of Windows, and you can change it in Settings.

Is there a version for Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Shift is made for Windows 10 and 11.

How do I uninstall Shift?

Go to Settings › Apps › Installed apps, find Shift and choose Uninstall. With winget: winget uninstall AmbulantBiv.Shift.
